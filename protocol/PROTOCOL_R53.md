# PROTOCOL R53 v1.0 — energy competition at the point of no return (FROZEN 2026-10-21)

**Frozen before any analysis of the second hold-out frames (SMF2B/2CG/4CG-NF/8CG/12B/12CG/20B/20CG).** Supersedes `PROTOCOL_R53_DRAFT.md`. The development set (SMF4B × FF) and the first hold-out (SMF4B × NF, SMF8B × FF+NF) have been analysed in the pilots; the thresholds below were set with that knowledge and are stated as such.

## 1. Definitions (unchanged from the draft unless noted)

- Models, records, IM, collapse history, ZIC, committed/recoverable, t_a, t\*, rest release, t\*_s, released state: as R52 / draft §1.
- **Remaining barrier W_rem(t_r):** first-mode-pattern displacement-controlled static push of the released configuration in the collapse direction (velocities discarded, GM pattern removed, gravity and P-Δ on); increments 0.002 in ×10 then 0.02 in; first-step ladder on failure (`driver53.barrier`); W(s) = ∫λ f·du from the released configuration; **W_rem = max_s W(s)** before λ returns to ≤ 0; W_rem = 0 if λ never becomes positive (downhill); a push that fails at step 0 is reported as *no barrier* (not imputed). Pattern sensitivity: `story` (block above the critical storey) and `shape` (collapse displacement increment) on the sensitivity subsets; pilot: W_story/W_mode1 median 0.999 (4B), 1.01 (8B).
- **Escape energy E_crit (damped):** β-scaling of the entire velocity/acceleration field at t_r with integrator restart; bisection to max(0.01, 2 %); E_crit = β²_crit·KE(t_r); censored above β = 8.
- **Escape energy E⁰_crit (undamped):** same with all Rayleigh damping (global and region-based) removed at t_r; hysteresis unchanged. Verified by free-vibration decay of the intact frame (< 2 % energy change over 10 cycles).
- **Dissipation toll:** D = E_crit − E⁰_crit; c = E_crit/W_rem; c⁰ = E⁰_crit/W_rem.
- **Development constant:** c_dev = 1.13 (median c over the 87 SMF4B×FF transition states, pilot). Frame constants c_f = median c over that frame's transition states are reported alongside; nothing else is fitted.
- **Erosion series:** pushes at t95−2, t95−1, t95, then t95+0.5k up to t_c−0.05 (≤ 40 per history; step widened uniformly if needed), plus t_a and t\*. **Last crossing** t_last: start of the final uninterrupted run of knots with max KE in [t_k−0.125, t_k+0.125] ≥ c_dev·W_rem(t_k). KE from the reference history (0.05 s). Comparison metric: t_last − t\*.
- **Static edge θ_edge:** peak storey drift at the λ = 0 return of the first-mode push.

**Reporting rule for failures:** if a frame misses H1a (median outside 0.90–1.05 or IQR width > 0.20), the law is reported as frame-dependent and the Discussion names multi-storey mechanism participation as the suspected cause, with the plastic-work decomposition of H6 as evidence; no threshold is moved after the fact.

## 4. Claim wording (binding)
- The law is stated for the undamped probe as an approximate equality with its interval; for damped frames as an inequality with a dissipation toll (frame-dependent median, interval reported).
- The classifier is a margin, not a certificate: fate is certain outside the band and decided by dissipation inside it.
- "Capacity decided in the coda" is replaced by: *at the collapse-threshold intensity the coda erodes most of the barrier that survives the strong motion; the collapse-capacity statistic is therefore sensitive to the coda* — with the erosion fractions and truncation bias as evidence.
- No "first", "validated", "universal"; model class and criteria named in every claim.

**A3 (2026-10-21, boundary-condition correction discovered by the second hold-out; no threshold moved).** The CG frames exposed a defect in the §1 definition of W_rem. On SMF4CG and SMF2CG the first-mode push keeps positive resistance well beyond the adopted collapse drift limit θ_c = 0.15 (median peak storey drift at the λ = 0 return: 0.17 for 4CG), so the frozen barrier integrates work the released state never has to perform before the dynamic label is decided: W_edge/W(θ_c) median = 1.75 (4CG), 3.15 (2CG). Recomputed against the work to reach the drift limit, the apparent CG anomaly disappeared (E_crit/W(θ_c) median ≈ 1.07). The definition is therefore amended:

- **W_rem^c(t_r)** = peak cumulative work W(s) up to the **earlier** of (i) the first λ ≤ 0 return after a positive λ (loss of effective resistance, the frozen edge) and (ii) the first crossing of the moment-frame peak storey drift through θ_c = 0.15, linearly interpolated between stored curve points. Pushes truncated by non-convergence / displacement cap before either edge keep the frozen convention W_rem^c = max W(s) of the recorded curve and are flagged `trunc`; downhill states keep W_rem^c = 0; first-step failures remain *no barrier*. The edge type (`drift` | `edge` | `trunc`) is reported per state.
- **Physical reading (binding wording).** W_rem^c is the quasi-static work from the released configuration to the **collapse surface** — the adopted limit state of the dynamic label — whether that surface is reached by loss of lateral resistance (edge-limited frames: SMF4B, SMF8B) or by the drift limit while still resisting (drift-limited frames: SMF2B, SMF2CG, SMF4CG; gravity-frame/composite resistance survives past θ_c).
- **Constants unchanged.** c_dev = 1.13 is unaffected (SMF4B is edge-limited; W_rem^c = W_rem there). All pre-set hypothesis thresholds stand.

**A12 (2026-10-27, post hoc analysis rule for the A6 residual-capacity analysis; decided after the 2B/4B/8B/4CG/8CG interim results were seen).** The retained-barrier ratio w = W_set / W_int is treated as a measured quantity only where both pushes reach an exit (drift, edge or stall under A3/A11) and the intact push is taken in the same direction as the settled push. Reasons: (i) by A3 a `trunc` push records a lower bound, not a barrier; (ii) `intact_symmetry.json` shows the composite frames are not mirror-symmetric (forward and backward intact load factors differ by 6–11 % of the peak over the common range; bare frames 0.1–0.5 %), so the opposite-direction intact barrier is not an exact normaliser. Interim evidence that motivated the rule, recorded before any change of text: on SMF4CG/SMF8CG, 95/387 and 97/275 settled pushes are `trunc`, almost all in the forward direction; with them included the residual drift ranks capacity better than w, with them excluded w ranks better. The all-states, mirror-normalised and truncated-only results are reported alongside the primary subset (Supplementary Table "sensitivity to the barrier-resolution rule"); no state is dropped silently.

**A13 (2026-10-27, post hoc repeat-run audit; no definitions changed).** To bound the sensitivity of the declared static push to solver noise, a sample of 604 transition-state pushes (weighted toward the composite variants; tstar/ta states on SMF2CG/4B/4CG/8CG/12CG/20B/20CG) was repeated twice from identical released states on the 192-thread server (frozen task fork_rate; outputs logs/fork_rate.csv). Audit classification: a push is 'deviating' when a repetition disagrees with the canonical run by more than 1e-6 relative work or changes the exit class; pushes with no barrier on all three runs are 'consistent no-barrier', not failures. Results: 0 of 53 completed bare-frame pushes deviated; 72 of 395 completed composite pushes deviated (median work deviation 0.01%, worst 4.6%, 4 exit-class flips); 19 repetitions and 15 canonical runs failed to reach an exit (2 of the canonical failures on bare 20B); 116 states produced no barrier on any repetition. The canonical run remains the measurement used in all tables; the repeats are audit-only. Numbers are emitted as fork* macros (analysis/make_numbers_fork.py; same block merged into make_numbers53.py).
